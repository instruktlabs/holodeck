# First-person conversation study

2026-10-21. Three generated images follow one task through the proposed Holodeck
experience. The creation-platform v3 image supplies the cove's identity.

1. **Discuss:** eye-level inside the cove. References stay on the rail; one
   explicit world-rule decision appears beside a short summary of the request.
2. **Direct:** at the open threshold, point at a stream crossing and request a
   bridge. The interface contracts to the relevant target, voice transcript,
   and one active task with Stop and Details.
3. **Review:** inspect the proposed bridge in context. Apply, revise, or discard
   it; the relevant reference and concise runtime evidence accompany the proposal.

The HTML walkthrough lets the reviewer move between these states with buttons
or arrow keys and view the images full-screen. It is a storyboard, not free
movement through a 3D scene, a live agent session, or a production UI. Evidence
checks shown in the images are sample product copy, not measured results.

Open `/walkthrough.html` through the local dev server. Images and prompts are
stored here; public copies are in `public/design/first-person/`. The built-in
Image Generator produced the images. No existing image was overwritten.

Review focus: whether the scale and placement of the conversation blocks feel
comfortable; whether the view remains clear while directing work; and whether
the relevant target and decision are obvious without reading a transcript.

Future interaction detail: movement remains available during voice input;
keyboard text focus releases pointer lock. A conversation block is deliberately
placed or presented in a compact view-relative tray, not continually made to
follow every small head movement. The exact spatial anchoring needs an interactive
prototype after this visual review.
