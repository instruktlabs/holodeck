Use case: ui-mockup. Create a polished four-panel product design study for HOLODECK, a local browser-based 3D world-building tool. One coherent design with four equally sized widescreen viewport panels arranged 2x2, labeled small 01 ARRIVE, 02 REFERENCES, 03 SHAPE, 04 LISTEN. Each panel depicts the SAME cozy primitive earthen shelter open onto a softly lit cedar clearing. Low curved clay wall, pale mineral floor, timber beams, warm indirect daylight, a small flush origin stone with one delicate amber ring. Clearly a digital construct: restrained fine construction lines dissolve at the edge into warm ivory emptiness; beautiful simplified geometry. No oven, furnace, science-fiction console, holographic neon or cyberpunk. Calm Japanese-influenced editorial interface, generous negative space, warm ivory translucent solid paper-like panels, charcoal elegant typography, amber selection. 01 ARRIVE: almost unobstructed space, small bottom prompt 'What shall we make?' and a subtle microphone control, small top left 'holodeck'. 02 REFERENCES: a beautifully proportioned floating reference board, two cedar clearing images, tiny 'Light' and 'Openness' annotations, quiet 'Pin direction' action; world remains visible. 03 SHAPE: one stone bench selected with a fine amber contour; compact nearby contextual card 'Stone bench', 'Position', 'Material', 'Revise'; no busy 3D gizmos. 04 LISTEN: same scene with a quiet listening pill and editable transcription 'Keep the path open to the trees.'; understated waveform and visible 'Stop'. Each viewport should read as credible desktop software, not a poster illustration. Keep world foreground/ground dominant, controls sparse, excellent legibility. Overall palette stone, sand, warm gray, charcoal, restrained moss, amber. Large high quality landscape image. This is a concept board, not a runtime screenshot.
