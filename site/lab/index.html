Medieval Castle

A fortress holding a mountain pass: a stone bridge over a gorge to a gatehouse between round towers, a curtain wall and a keep. The B6 rebuild in progress, then the earlier B5 pass, along the same 20-second camera path.

In progress. B6 is a partial assembly for a showing: saved modules beside unsaved draft gate towers and keep, grey stand-ins and a river sheet floating above the gorge. A Unity build of B6 is still to come.

Firearms Through Time: Colt M4 at the range

A first-person range game whose rules run in two engines from shared fixtures. The same M4 sequence in Unreal and in the browser: aim, a semi round, a held 3-round burst, a short pull the cam remembers, inspect, reload, bursts until dry, and the empty reload.

In progress. Playable here: F or click fires, X takes a gun off the wall rack. Placeholder range art, Unreal still shows its test readout, and the reload is mostly below the view.
