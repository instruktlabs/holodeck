Firearms Through Time: Colt M4 at the range

A first-person range game whose rules run in two engines from shared fixtures. The same M4 sequence in Unreal and in the browser: aim, a semi round, a held 3-round burst, a short pull the cam remembers, inspect, reload, bursts until dry, and the empty reload.

Status In progress. Playable here: F or click fires, X takes a gun off the wall rack. Placeholder range art, Unreal still shows its test readout, and the reload is mostly below the view.
