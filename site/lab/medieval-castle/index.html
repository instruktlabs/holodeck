Medieval Castle

A fortress holding a mountain pass: a stone bridge over a gorge to a gatehouse between round towers, a curtain wall and a keep. The B6 rebuild in progress, then the earlier B5 pass, along the same 20-second camera path.

Play the in-browser buildWatch B6 in R3F (WebGPU), B5 in R3F (WebGPU), B5 in Unity

Status In progress. B6 is a partial assembly for a showing: saved modules beside unsaved draft gate towers and keep, grey stand-ins and a river sheet floating above the gorge. A Unity build of B6 is still to come.
