# Creation platform and cove identities

Owner clarification, 2026-10-21. This supersedes the generic cabin presentation
and any assumption that the initial world is already a forest or landscape.

## Start in the construct

Holodeck opens on a bounded platform within a quiet, unmade grid construct.
A cozy cove and recognizable Kiln instrument sit on that platform. The grid
communicates empty authorable space; the cove is a familiar place to think,
collect references, talk to the coding agent, and make the world bible.

The surrounding world begins empty. It takes shape from the conversation,
references, and accepted rules. A finished landscape must not be hardcoded into
the starter identity. There can be multiple cove presets, each with its own
cohesive material and shape language and a consistent role for the Kiln core.

## Authored identity kits

The platform, cove shell, Kiln core, seats, canopy, reference support, and lights
are original reusable assets authored in a separate Kiln workspace. Deliveries
retain editable source, stable IDs, exact saved revisions, placement anchors,
collision policy, and resource budgets. Primitive geometry in the compatibility
lab is test equipment, not an accepted cove asset or production art direction.

A cove preset is a versioned assembly of those asset revisions. The platform
defines a clear local frame and supports the starter safely. The Kiln core is
the recognizable creative instrument at its heart; its final shape is still a
visual decision. Do not substitute an industrial furnace or arbitrary stone
without reviewing the identity.

## Authoring and inhabiting

Mood-board references, world-bible decisions, conversation, selection, and
progress are accessible in the creation space. Text remains comfortable to edit
on desktop even when represented by spatial surfaces. An image reference can
influence a named object, region, or rule, with the exact revision recorded.

The user speaks or types to their coding agent. That agent plans, authors,
observes, and refines the world through Holodeck's interface and tools. If the
chosen agent later delegates building work, the visible session still owns a
coherent conversation and coordinates one serialized stream of scene commits.
Multiple builder agents are not required for the first prototype.

The origin anchor, grid, platform/cove assembly, authoring surfaces, and created
world are independent layers. Entering the world can hide the entire starter
assembly, including its colliders, lights, and sound. Hide only after a valid
world spawn/support surface is ready; do not drop the visitor through the ground.
Summoning the cove checks placement and occupancy and offers a safe return.
Neither action deletes the surrounding world, its bible, or its references.

## Visual review sequence

1. Choose the platform silhouette, cove kit identity, and Kiln core shape.
2. Review arrival in the empty grid, reference/bible use, listening, and world
   creation beyond the platform.
3. Review entering the world with the complete starter hidden and summoning it again.
4. Author the selected kit in Kiln, then compare actual runtime captures against
   the selected concept. Concept approval does not qualify collision or performance.

The first generic cabin study was rejected. The cove-kit study clarified asset
identity but predated the explicit platform/grid starting state. The platform
study is the current candidate, not an approved design.
