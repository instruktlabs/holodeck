## Selected identity and configuration

Use **Colt M4 Carbine R0977 as pictured in the 2024 manufacturer catalog**, with ribbed two-piece handguard, detachable carrying handle/rear sight, fixed front sight, sliding stock and curved metal magazine. The longer outer envelope will be interpreted with the stock extended. Do not mix in the neighboring R0977HB-INT quad rail, contemporary PMAG or an optic. This is an exterior interpretation of that catalog configuration, not a replica of a museum accession or a verified issued specimen.

Era: late twentieth-century M4 family, retained as a legacy catalog product in 2024. Colt's timeline records military adoption in 1994; that is a family milestone, not an assertion that every pictured component existed in that year. The exact catalog configuration's introduction and service dates remain uncertain.

## Authored versus observed

Observed: major silhouette relationships, receiver/stock/foreend/magazine grouping, dark exterior material separation, open carry-handle and sight-frame silhouettes, curved magazine and ribbed polymer surfaces. Authored estimates: cross-sections, bevels, underside, small control relief, surface roughness, stock extension pose, visual hand targets and every internal cavity depth.

The visible muzzle will have a real open mouth, rim and receding inner walls ending in a **blind artistic cavity**, not a through-bore or functional construction. The profile reference shows vented muzzle furniture, but does not qualify the exact front face, slot layout or cavity depth. A closer exact-configuration muzzle reference remains a historical-fidelity gate even if the geometric opening passes review.

## Asset and attachment contract

Metres, +X forward, +Y up, +Z right; origin in the receiver. Named stable groups: receiver, stock, handguard, magazine, carry handle, barrel/muzzle and exterior controls. Named visual targets: primary/support grips, right/left hands, rear/front sight line, muzzle FX, magazine interaction and receiver-top mount. Anchor presence is not proof of hand fit or runtime support.

The detachable carry handle is the sole included mount demonstration and is installed by default. Its own mount origin must coincide with the receiver mount anchor in the authored pose. The proposed asset-local allowlist contains only this authored carry-handle configuration; it has not been registered in the game. No arbitrary Picatinny optic, suppressor, laser, modern foregrip, launcher or unrelated grip is accepted; a receiver rail is not a universal compatibility grant. Removing the handle and installing another accessory requires its own referenced candidate and destination validation. No live attachment-contract file is changed.

All modeling and materials will be original procedural artwork. No reference pixels, logos, serial numbers, historic inscriptions, borrowed meshes, texture scans or manufacturer engineering dimensions are used in the GLB. Local study images are not part of the asset package. Rights for public use of the model name/trademarks and reference reproduction remain distinct from original geometry authorship.
