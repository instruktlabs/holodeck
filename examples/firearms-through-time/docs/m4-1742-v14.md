# M4 and Pattern 1742 v14: held triggers, a burst, and a flintlock lock

27 October 2026. Roadmap item 3: bring the Colt M4 (R0977) and the Pattern 1742 musket to playable in both hosts, each with its own recoil profile. The M4 brings the first burst fire.

Owner decisions (question tool, 27 Oct, all the recommended options):

- **M4 modes:** semi and a 3-round burst. B switches modes. The burst runs at about 800 rounds a minute (an Estimate). The engine also supports full auto, which the M4 doesn't use.
- **Burst cam:** the real non-resetting cam. Letting go mid-burst leaves the cam where it is, and the next pull fires only the rest. The HUD shows the count.
- **1742 load:** a condensed drill of about 10 s. The ramrod is its own part (gun revision v3) and there is a paper-cartridge cue.
- **1742 lock:** lock delay, no misfires. The pan flashes first and the shot leaves 60 ms after the trigger, wherever the muzzle points at ignition.
- **Kiln:** upgraded to 1.6.0 for this round.

## How the trigger behaves

`game/trigger.mjs` and `game/fire-frame.mjs` hold the rules; `native/.../WeaponTrigger.h` is the Unreal port of the same code.

- **Rounds fall on the trigger's own clock, not per frame.** A held trigger fires at exact times: the last round plus the cycle (0.075 s on the M4). One frame can hold several rounds. Each frame is split at those times, and the gun, the trigger clock and the recoil step together.
- **Each round leaves along the aim at its own moment.** That includes the kick turned earlier in the same frame, clamped like the host's look. So a burst lands the same at 30 fps and at 250 fps.
- **A pull is latched.** A click shorter than a frame still fires once. A refused round (empty, reloading, inspecting) ends the pull and leaves the burst cam where it was.
- **Semi:** one round per pull. **Burst:** while the trigger is down, up to three rounds a cycle apart. The cam counts 0, 1, 2 and doesn't reset on release, so the next pull finishes the burst.
- **Flintlock:** the pull drops the lock and commits the round (the gun and the range are asked here). The pan flashes for 40 ms. The ignition 60 ms later sends the ball along the aim at that moment and starts the kick.
- **The M4's empty reload is longer:** 2.9 s against 2.3 s, with the charging handle after the seat. The magazine seats at 62% of the standard reload and 50% of the empty one. Ammo, reserve and conservation are as before: capacity counts the magazine only.

## Proof that both hosts agree

- **Shared fixtures:** `game/trigger-fixtures-v1.json` holds four scenarios: M4 semi, M4 burst cam, M4 empty burst and reload, and musket lock. They are replayed with frames cut at each input's exact time, at 30, 90, 144 (uneven splits) and 251 Hz. Every event must match its time within 1e-9 s and its aim within 1e-9°.
  - **Browser rules:** `game/trigger.test.mjs` replays them.
  - **Native rules:** the header-only port compiled outside Unreal (`pipeline/check-trigger-native.ps1`, MSVC 14.51.36231). Result: 88 events, worst 7.98e-13 s and 2.4e-14°.
  - **Inside the engine:** the same replay runs as part of each WeaponLab run.
- **Faults are caught:** `pipeline/trigger-negative-tests.mjs` (browser rules) and `check-trigger-native.ps1 -Negative` (native) each break one rule at a time in a copy. The browser rules catch 7 of 7 faults and the native 8 of 8: burst cam resets, burst runs past three, cycle ignored, flintlock without delay, events at frame start, in-frame turn ignored, empty reload on the standard clip, and (native only) magazine seating at the end.
  - The native pass found that no fixture told the 2.9 s empty reload from the 2.3 s one. A pull at 4.0 s, which only the longer reload refuses, now makes the difference visible.
- **Shadowing:** the standalone compile now treats variable shadowing (C4456–C4459) as an error, like the engine build. It failed on the old fixture header, where a local `Rules` hid the generated `Rules[]` table.

## Colt M4

- **Gun:** v3 proportions, Kiln revision `r_f0ad6458af8b40c48ca99eca88d48abe` (959,976 bytes, sha256 `d91af29c…`). The magazine, charging handle, front post and rear aperture are separate nodes.
- **Hands:** new asset `a_960aeb0b45694d409e3f0b8bfb1863f6`, revision `r_19df90fcdb034e58ad345c705bfa2b42` (12,883,496 bytes, sha256 `8abcb4b5…`), 41 bones. They were rebuilt from the new arm rig `game/carbine-m4-arm-rig-v1.json`; the right shoulder moved out and back for the pistol grip, and both wrists are straight at Ready.
- **Motion:** `game/carbine-m4-motion-v1.json`, 26 channels. Clips: Ready 1 s, Inspect 3.6 s, Fire 0.2 s (held while the trigger is down), Reload 2.3 s and ReloadEmpty 2.9 s.
- **Reload:**
  - The index straightens onto the magazine release, and the magazine drops along its well axis and hides once clear.
  - The left hand draws a fresh magazine (a copy of the gun's own) and inserts it along the same axis. The gun's magazine rides up with it on the same two keys, so they coincide all the way in, and only the copy hides at the commit.
  - The empty reload adds the left hand reaching over the receiver to pull the charging handle.
- **Measured grip at Ready** (evidence/carbine-m4-hands-v1/): every pad 0.5–1.7 mm off the gun on the correct side. The right index sits on the trigger face, the right fingers wrap the front strap and the thumb is on the left. The left fingers are on the far side of the handguard and the thumb on the near side.
  - **Penetration:** none at Ready on the right and 0.32 mm on the left; 0.62 mm at worst through the clips.
  - **Reach:** no arm stretches.
  - **Negative run:** the same test fails on the No.4 hands posed on the M4, as required.
  - **Magazine handover:** the copy is 0.0006 mm and 0.0001° off the seated magazine at the keys before the commit.
- **Not met:**
  - **Charging-handle travel is 63 mm, not the 70 mm Estimate.** The stock overlaps the handle by 1.10 mm at 70 mm, and 63 mm is the longest pull within 1 mm.
  - **The index returns to the trigger face after pressing the release.** The single-axis index can't leave the guard and come back without crossing the receiver. A real shooter keeps it straight along the receiver.
  - At full pull the left elbow sits 18 cm ahead of the wrist; this shows only from the side.
- **Seen in both hosts:** the magazine change and the charging-handle pull happen mostly below the game view. The authoring sheets used a wider camera (FOV 95). See "Not done".

## Browser (Holodeck)

- **Controls:** F or LMB pulls (hold for the burst), release lets go. B switches semi and burst. The HUD shows the mode and the rounds left in the burst.
- **Observed on the M4, WebGPU, in my own tab:**
  - **Fire modes:** semi fires one round per press. In burst, holding F fires exactly three rounds and stops. Releasing after two rounds leaves "BURST 1/3", and the next pull fires one.
  - **Reload:** it starts at 23 rounds. The magazine seats at 1.427 s (62% of 2.3 s): ammo 30/30, reserve 83. The gun is ready at 2.301 s.
- **Forced WebGL2** (`?backend=webgl`; the canvas holds a WebGL2 context): the same sequence gives the same counts. The magazine seats at 1.430 s and the gun is ready at 2.306 s. The tab ran at about 73 fps with Unreal under load on the same PC (DIRTY).

  - **Trigger fixture in the engine:** 88 events, worst 7.98e-13 s and 2.4e-14°.
  - **Recoil fixture:** 15 kicks and 141 aim samples, worst 2.66e-15°.
  - **The checks** cover:
    - the empty pull that starts no string, the aim and sight alignment, and semi;
    - a held burst of exactly three, the mid-burst release that keeps the cam, and the next pull finishing the burst;
    - inspect and reload refusing rounds;
    - the magazine dropping, hiding, riding in the left hand and seating at the commit;
